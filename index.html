How marks work

KPI 1 · Daily tasks (50)
  • 5 tasks × 10 marks. Done ÷ target × 10, max 10 each.
  • All check-ins must be done between 10am and 9pm.
  • Attendance: sign in by 12pm with a selfie, and sign out between 6pm and 9pm, 31 days
  • Appointments: 2 a day with client names. Extra ones don’t add marks.
  • Daily reel: counts only with the reel link
  • 10am learning video: upload the video in the app, Mon–Fri, 22 weekdays
  • YouTube: 1 link a week, target 4
  • MC days (max 3) need a reason and doctor’s letter, and come off the targets.
  • Group or team KPI 1 = average of all its members.
KPI 2 · Sales (50)
  • Add all members' verified sales.
  • In each area, the team with higher sales gets 50.
  • The other team: its sales ÷ top team's sales × 50.
  • Groups are scored the same way against the top group in their area.
  • Cancelled sales don't count.
Winner
  • Team A vs Team B in each area. Higher KPI 1 + KPI 2 (out of 100) wins.
  • Check-in runs 1–31 October only.
