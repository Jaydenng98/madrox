How marks work

KPI 1 · Daily tasks (50)
  • 5 tasks × 10 marks. Done ÷ target × 10, max 10 each.
  • Check-in is open 9am–9pm: learning video 9–10am, everything else from 10am.
  • Office presence: sign in (with a selfie) and sign out, both between 10am and 6pm, 31 days
  • Appointments: 2 a day with client names. Extra ones don’t add marks.
  • Reels: 1 a day with the link, Monday to Friday only, 22 weekdays
  • Learning video: share it with your group, then check in on the app between 9am and 10am, every day, 31 days
  • YouTube: 1 link a week, target 4
  • MC days (max 3) need a reason and doctor’s letter, and come off the targets.
  • Group or team KPI 1 = average of all its members.
KPI 2 · Bookings (50)
  • Count all members’ done-deal bookings. Each booking = 1, whatever the price. Closed 2 bookings? Enter 2 in “How many”.
  • Based only on the number of valid bookings, not price, SPA, commission or booking fee. Every booking counts equally.
  • KPI 2 = 50 \u00d7 (1 \u2212 0.8bookings): 1 = 10, 2 = 18, 3 = 24.4, 5 = 33.6, 10 = 44.6. 15 or more bookings = full 50.
  • Group and team KPI 2 = average of their members\u2019 KPI 2.
  • Cancelled bookings don’t count.
Winner
  • Team A vs Team B in each area. Higher KPI 1 + KPI 2 (out of 100) wins.
  • Check-in runs 1–31 October only.
