How marks work

KPI 1 · Daily tasks (50)
  • 5 tasks × 10 marks. Done ÷ target × 10, max 10 each.
  • Learning video 9–10am. Appointments, reel, YouTube and bookings 10am–11:59pm.
  • Office presence: Monday to Friday only, 22 days. Post the group photo in the group chat, then tap Sign in between 9:45am and 10:00am. Tap Sign out any time before 11:59pm. Both are needed.
  • Appointments: 2 a day with client names, every day (target 62). Submit each appointment on its own. Log every appointment you have; 2 a day count for marks. Every appointment = 1: just add the time, client name and project.
  • Reels: 1 a day with the link (its own submit), Monday to Friday only, 22 days
  • Learning video: share it with your group, then check in on the app between 9am and 10am, every day, 31 days
  • YouTube: 1 link a week (Shorts or full video), 4 weeks: 5–11, 12–18, 19–25 and 26–31 Oct, target 4
  • MC days (max 3) need a reason (send the doctor\u2019s letter to Alicia), and come off the targets.
  • Group or team KPI 1 = average of all its members.
KPI 2 · Bookings (50)
  • 1 full deal = 1 mark. Everyone on the deal logs their own part and types their own % (their commission split). 60% = 0.6 mark.
  • Tick your role (closer, caller, ads) and write who you worked with. Did it all yourself? Type 100.
  • Based only on the number of valid bookings, not price, SPA, commission or booking fee.
  • KPI 2 = all your deal marks added up, maximum 50.
  • Group and team KPI 2 = average of their members\u2019 KPI 2.
  • Cancelled bookings don’t count.
Winner
  • Team A vs Team B in each area. Higher KPI 1 + KPI 2 (out of 100) wins.
  • Check-in runs 1–31 October only.
